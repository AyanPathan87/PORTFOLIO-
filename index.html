Who I Am

The short version: hardware kid who leads by building alongside people, not just for them.

I'm a second-year Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering student at Government College of Engineering, Nagpur, currently holding an 8.09 CGPA across two semesters. My focus is embedded systems and IoT — microcontroller programming with Arduino, ESP8266 and ESP32, basic circuit and PCB design, and the wireless protocols that tie smart devices together.

Outside the lab, I lead the College E-Yantra Robotics Club as President, where I design workshops, procure hardware kits, and get first-year students soldering and coding within their first semester. I've organized a 3-day RC Modelling workshop for 75+ students across colleges, a 7-day IoT program, and multiple Arduino sessions — because the fastest way I learn something is by teaching it next.

I also compete: my team fielded an upgraded bot for the Cozmoclench challenge at Techfest IIT Bombay's Nagpur zonal, placed 2nd at the Avishkar 4.0 inter-college project competition, and got selected at college level for Smart India Hackathon 2025. Tools like TinkerCAD, Proteus, Canva and a working knowledge of C/C++, Python and MATLAB round out the toolkit.
